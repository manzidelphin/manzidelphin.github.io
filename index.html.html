My Journey

From Idea to Product

How GymMind went from a problem I noticed to a live SaaS platform serving gyms in Rwanda.

The Problem
Identifying the Gap
I noticed that gyms in Rwanda were managing members with paper records, WhatsApp messages, and manual spreadsheets. There was no affordable, purpose-built software for African gyms.
The Vision
Designing GymMind
I designed a full-stack platform covering every aspect of gym management — from member check-ins to corporate billing. The focus: simple, powerful, and built for Rwanda.
The Build
Full-Stack Development
Built the entire platform solo — backend API with Node.js/TypeScript, React frontend, MySQL database, and deployment on a Hetzner VPS with Nginx and PM2.
The Launch
Live at gymmind.rw
GymMind went live serving FitLife Kigali and partner gyms, with institutional billing for companies like MMI and real-time SMS notifications for members.
The Future
Scaling Across Africa
Adding MTN MoMo payments, AI analytics, multi-gym support, and expanding to more gyms across East Africa. GymMind is just getting started.
